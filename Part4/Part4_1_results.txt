CONVENTIONS: decimal returns internally; monthly rebalancing, zero costs.
Annual arithmetic mean = 12*monthly mean; CAGR is a separate compounded growth rate.
Annual volatility = sqrt(12)*sample monthly SD (ddof=1). These annualizations do not adjust for serial dependence.
RF annual = 1%; RF monthly = (1.01)^(1/12)-1 = 0.000829538 decimal (0.082954%).
Annual Sharpe = sqrt(12)*mean(R-RF_monthly)/SD(R). Initial wealth of 1 is included in drawdowns.

P3 Three bonds: annual arithmetic return difference +0.74 pp;
  CAGR difference +0.82 pp; volatility difference -0.26 pp;
  drawdown -22.45% versus -27.11%; Sharpe 0.626 versus 0.548.
  P3 replaces long-duration Treasuries with aggregate (AGG), high-yield (JNK) and investment-grade corporate bonds (LQD). Duration and credit risks differ.

P4 Four equities/four bonds: annual arithmetic return difference -1.35 pp;
  CAGR difference -1.45 pp; volatility difference +0.17 pp;
  drawdown -26.78% versus -27.11%; Sharpe 0.426 versus 0.548.
  P4 spreads equity across four regions/size exposures and bonds across all four bond ETFs; TLT falls to 10% of the portfolio.

P5 Equity factors: annual arithmetic return difference -0.84 pp;
  CAGR difference -0.88 pp; volatility difference -0.13 pp;
  drawdown -29.12% versus -27.11%; Sharpe 0.481 versus 0.548.
  P5 holds momentum, size, value and minimum-volatility equity tilts; these remain equity exposures, with 40% TLT unchanged.

P6 Alternatives: annual arithmetic return difference +3.35 pp;
  CAGR difference +3.45 pp; volatility difference +1.23 pp;
  drawdown -21.97% versus -27.11%; Sharpe 0.755 versus 0.548.
  P6 replaces bonds with REITs (USRT), a hedge-strategy tracker (QAI), infrastructure (IGF) and listed private equity (PSP). These are not bond substitutes with guaranteed protection.

P7 Equal weight: annual arithmetic return difference -0.36 pp;
  CAGR difference -0.35 pp; volatility difference -0.35 pp;
  drawdown -23.40% versus -27.11%; Sharpe 0.533 versus 0.548.
  P7 holds 25% regional/broad equities, 25% equity factor ETFs, 25% bond ETFs and 25% alternatives. It is not a 60/40 allocation; alternatives can carry equity-like risk.

Exposures offer plausible reasons for differences: regional equity performance,
interest-rate sensitivity of long-duration Treasuries, corporate credit risk,
factor tilts and equity-like alternative risks. This comparison does not isolate causal effects.
Highest observed Sharpe: P6 Alternatives; highest price CAGR: P6 Alternatives.
Smallest drawdown magnitude: P6 Alternatives (-21.97%);
largest: P2 Regional equities (-32.25%).
Holding more ETFs did not automatically establish better diversification; assess
the actual volatility and drawdown, not just the number of holdings.
For retirement, large losses can be especially damaging when withdrawals force
sales during a downturn. This growth-of-1 exercise has no contributions or
withdrawals and does not simulate retirement withdrawal outcomes.
The common period excludes earlier market episodes; observed rankings are not
guarantees of future performance or a personalized portfolio recommendation.
Most importantly, the file does NOT document dividend-adjusted or total-return
prices. Results are labelled PRICE returns; omitted distributions could materially
change CAGR, Sharpe and rankings, especially for bond, REIT and income-oriented ETFs.
No dividends are invented. Splits/other adjustment conventions are also undocumented.
