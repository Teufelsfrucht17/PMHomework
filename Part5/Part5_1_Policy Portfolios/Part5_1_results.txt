HYPOTHETICAL CHOICE TODAY
For a hypothetical investor with a genuine 30-year horizon, separate emergency
savings and tolerance for substantial fluctuations, my illustrative preference is
60% equity / 40% risk-free initially: retain substantial growth exposure while
starting with a lower volatility profile than all-equity in this historical analysis.

It does not maintain a 60/40 risk profile, guarantee a floor, or maximize every investor's utility.

average terminal balance is not automatically best. A September 2026 investment's
